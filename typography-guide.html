Content Checklist

  • Map marketing copy to type-display or type-heading-1 depending on hierarchy.
  • Prefer type-body for paragraphs. Use type-body-strong instead of manually toggling font-semibold.
  • Wrap decorative emojis or icons with aria-hidden when they have adjacent text.
  • Ensure every page exposes a skip link target via the main-content id.
